A Buyer's Overview

About Hero Homes The Hemisphere at Sector 27

Luxury Golf-Side Living Redefined at Greater Noida

Some addresses give you a home. Others give you a way of living. Hero Homes The Hemisphere belongs firmly to the second group. Set in Sector 27, right beside the established Jaypee Greens belt and only minutes from Pari Chowk, this 100+ acre integrated township is designed around one simple idea: your home should open onto green, not onto traffic.

Wake up to a view of manicured fairways. Walk to a clubhouse for breakfast. Spend the evening on a tennis court, in a pool or on a quiet garden path, then drive back to a city that is only a few minutes away. At The Hemisphere, the resort lifestyle is not a weekend escape. It is your everyday address.

Total Land Parcel 100+ Acres
Open Green Space ~80% Greens
Typologies Suites, Apts & Villas
Clubhouse 50,000+ sq ft

The contrast with conventional city living is striking. Most urban housing today means towers packed close together, balconies that face other balconies, and open space measured in tiny pockets. The Hemisphere takes the opposite approach. With a low-density master plan spread over more than 100 acres and roughly 80% of the land kept as landscaped greens, every residence enjoys light, air, privacy and a view. Open space is the main feature here, not the leftover.

The township brings together golf suites, 2, 3 and 4 BHK luxury apartments and independent golf villas, so families at different stages of life can find a home that fits. Young professionals can choose compact golf suites. Growing families can opt for spacious three and four bedroom residences. Those who want complete privacy can choose a villa with its own garden and plunge pool.

Beyond the homes themselves, the township is built for comfort. A vehicle-free pedestrian network keeps walking paths safe and calm. Golf carts handle internal mobility. A retail high street keeps daily needs close. And an ultra-luxury clubhouse, together with sports arenas, gives the community a natural place to gather.

The location adds to the appeal. Sector 27 is a mature, well-connected part of Greater Noida with the Delta 1 Metro Station and Pari Chowk close by, and with quick access to the Noida-Greater Noida Expressway and the Yamuna Expressway. Education, healthcare and shopping are all within a short drive. You get the quiet of a resort and the convenience of a fully developed neighbourhood.

Hero Homes The Hemisphere is developed by Hero Realty, part of the respected Hero Enterprise group, a name associated with trust, engineering discipline and a long-term view of wellbeing. For buyers who want to live well and invest wisely, this is a community worth a closer look.

Flexible Payment Plans & Cost Components

Buying a home should feel clear and manageable. The Hemisphere offers payment structures designed to suit different buyer preferences:

Construction-Linked Payment (CLP) Plan: Under the CLP plan, you pay in stages linked to the progress of construction. This spreads your outlay across the building timeline and keeps payments tied to visible progress on site. It is a popular choice for buyers who plan to use a home loan.

Down Payment Incentives: Buyers who prefer to pay a larger share upfront may be eligible for special down payment benefits and discounts. Ask our team about the current schemes available.

Other Cost Components: In addition to the basic sale price, buyers should plan for the following:

  • IFMS (Interest-Free Maintenance Security): a one-time refundable or adjustable deposit as per the agreement
  • Car Parking: covered parking charges per slot, as applicable
  • Club Membership Charges: one-time membership for the golf clubhouse and sports facilities
  • GST: as applicable under prevailing regulations
  • Registration and Stamp Duty: payable at the time of registration as per Uttar Pradesh government norms

For a personalised cost sheet and the latest offers, tap Get Quote and our advisors will walk you through the full breakdown, with no hidden surprises.

Resort-Inspired Living

World-Class Golf & Lifestyle Amenities

At The Hemisphere, amenities are not an afterthought. They are the way of life. Every facility has been planned to give you reasons to step outside, stay active and spend time with the people you care about.

Golf & Sports Arena

For sports lovers, the township is a playground. The centrepiece is a 9-hole golf course, supported by a driving range and a putting green for practice sessions. Beyond golf, you will find tennis courts, squash courts, a cricket practice net and a basketball court, with something for every age and skill level. Whether you are chasing a low handicap or simply enjoy a weekend game with friends, everything you need is a short walk from home.

Grand Clubhouse & Entertainment

The 50,000+ sq. ft. clubhouse is the social heart of the community. Enjoy meals at the fine-dining restaurant, unwind at the cigar lounge, catch a film in the mini-theatre or meet friends over coffee. The banquet hall is ready for birthdays, anniversaries and family celebrations, so you can host without leaving the township.

Wellness & Relaxation

True luxury includes taking care of yourself. Swim laps in the infinity swimming pool, or use the temperature-controlled indoor pool in any season. Relax at the luxury spa and sauna, stretch and breathe at the yoga pavilion, or stroll through the reflexology garden. Together these spaces support a calmer, healthier daily routine.

Smart Infrastructure & Security

Comfort is also about what you do not have to worry about. The township offers EV charging stations for a future-ready lifestyle, a 3-tier digital access control system for safety, high-speed elevators in the towers, 100% power backup and rainwater harvesting for responsible water management. Together with multi-level security and smart home automation, these features make daily life secure, convenient and sustainable.

Capital Appreciation Drivers

Why Invest in Hero Homes The Hemisphere?

A home here is a lifestyle choice, and it can also be a well-considered investment. Several factors come together to make The Hemisphere an appealing proposition for end-users and investors alike.

Rare Golf-Township Asset Class

Golf-facing homes are scarce, and scarcity supports value. Golf-facing properties have historically commanded a valuation and rental premium, commonly cited in the range of 20–35%, over comparable non-golf homes. Permanent views of green fairways, low-density planning and premium amenities all contribute to long-term desirability. (Premium ranges vary by market and are not guaranteed.)

Pari Chowk Core Micro-Market

Sector 27 is a mature neighbourhood with established social infrastructure, including schools, hospitals, retail and metro connectivity already in place. For buyers, this means reduced development risk and a community that is ready to live in rather than one that is still waiting for essentials to arrive.

Jewar Airport & Film City Catalyst

The upcoming Noida International Airport at Jewar and planned economic activity in the wider region are expected to bring new jobs, businesses and visitors. With about 25 minutes of transit via the Yamuna Expressway, The Hemisphere is positioned to benefit from rental demand from corporate professionals and expatriates seeking quality housing near these growth drivers.
