How Much Does a Home Cost?

Prices at Hero Leafsong Retreat start from ₹1.89 Cr* for the 3 BHK + 2 Toilets layout (1,650 sq ft). The 3 BHK + 3 Toilets layout (1,800 sq ft) starts from ₹2.07 Cr*, and the 3 BHK + Servant Room layout (2,000 sq ft) starts from ₹2.30 Cr*. These figures are indicative and work out to about ₹11,450 per sq ft of super built-up area.

Carpet area is lower than super built-up area, so compare projects on carpet area as well. Here it is 1,180 sq ft, 1,360 sq ft and 1,580 sq ft for the three layouts respectively.

The listed price may not be the final amount you pay. GST, stamp duty, registration, maintenance deposits, parking and any preferential location charges (PLC) for floor or view are usually charged on top. Ask for the full cost sheet and the payment plan in writing before you book.

To check whether the price is fair, compare the per-sq-ft rate, carpet area, possession date (listed as 2031 onwards*) and amenities with two or three other projects in Greater Noida. You can request the latest price list from the sales team at any time.

Reading the Floor Plans

The Hero Leafsong Retreat floor plans cover three 3 BHK layouts. All three have three bedrooms; they differ in size and in what extra space you get, either a third toilet or a separate servant room.

Choose by how you live. The 1,650 sq ft layout suits a family that wants three bedrooms without extra area to maintain. The 1,800 sq ft layout adds a third toilet, which helps when several family members share the home. The 2,000 sq ft layout adds a servant room and a fourth balcony for households that want domestic help on site.

When you view a plan, check where the kitchen, the living area and the balconies sit relative to the bedrooms, and ask which towers and floors have each layout available.

Where Is Hero Leafsong Retreat and What Is Nearby?

Hero Leafsong Retreat is in Sector MU, Greater Noida. Greater Noida is a planned city in Uttar Pradesh with wide roads, universities and growing business areas, and the distances below show what is within reach of the project.
