About Hero Homes DMIC Greater Noida

DMIC Integrated Industrial Township Development

Hero Homes DMIC is Hero Realty's residential development planned inside the Integrated Industrial Township, Greater Noida (IITGN) — a roughly 747-acre township developed as a joint venture between the Greater Noida Industrial Development Authority (GNIDA) and the National Industrial Corridor Development and Implementation Trust (NICDIT), under the Delhi-Mumbai Industrial Corridor (DMIC) programme. Hero's residential component covers 17.3 acres within this township, with a stated development potential of nearly 6 million sq. ft.

Unlike a standalone residential launch, Hero Homes DMIC sits inside a planned, government-backed industrial and logistics hub that already has manufacturing units operational and more being allotted — which is a different investment thesis from a purely residential micro-market like Sector MU. Buyers here are typically weighing long-term appreciation tied to industrial and employment growth in the corridor, not just residential demand.

The project features confirmed premium 3 BHK apartment configurations in three spacious sizes (1650 sq ft, 1900 sq ft, and 2200 sq ft), while the specific tower count and exact unit numbers are yet to be officially announced. For a design reference, Hero Realty's existing project — Hero Homes Sector 104 in Gurugram, on Dwarka Expressway — reflects the brand's general approach to layouts and amenities, though the Greater Noida floor plans may differ once released.

Where is Hero Homes DMIC Located?

Hero Homes DMIC sits inside the Integrated Industrial Township, Greater Noida (IITGN) — a government-planned node of the Delhi-Mumbai Industrial Corridor. The township directly abuts the Eastern Peripheral Expressway, with the Yamuna Expressway and Noida-Greater Noida Expressway providing further connectivity across the wider Greater Noida region. It's located roughly 11 km from Pari Chowk, close to the Boraki multi-modal transit hub (~4 km) and the Dadri logistics hub (~6 km), with Noida International Airport at Jewar approximately 40 km away.

Because this is an industrial-township address rather than a standalone residential sector, connectivity here is built around freight, logistics, and employment corridors as much as passenger travel — worth stating plainly rather than borrowing "X mins to the mall" framing from a purely residential project.
