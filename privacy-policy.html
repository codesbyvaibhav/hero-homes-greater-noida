This Privacy Policy explains how herohomenoida.com ("this website," "we," "us") collects, uses, discloses, and safeguards the personal information of visitors who browse this site or submit an enquiry about Hero Homes Sector MU, Greater Noida. By using this website, submitting a form, or contacting us by phone or WhatsApp, you agree to the practices described below.

1. Who we are

This website is operated by an authorized channel partner promoting Hero Homes Sector MU, Greater Noida, a project developed by Hero Realty (Hero Enterprise). We act as an independent intermediary that collects and forwards enquiries to the developer's authorized sales team for the purpose of providing project information, pricing, and booking assistance.

2. Information we collect

Information you provide directly: full name, phone number, email address, preferred apartment configuration (3 BHK + 2 Toilets, 3 BHK + 3 Toilets, or 3 BHK + Servant Room), site-visit preferences, and any comments you share with our team.

Information collected automatically: IP address, browser and device type, pages viewed, referring website, approximate location, and cookie/advertising identifiers.

3. How we use your information

We use your information to respond to enquiries, share brochures and cost sheets, arrange site visits, pass your details to the authorized sales team, send project updates and offers where permitted, run analytics and remarketing campaigns, and prevent spam or misuse of our forms. Processing is based on your consent and our legitimate interest in responding to genuine property enquiries, in line with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) and other applicable Indian law.

4. Who we share data with

We do not sell your personal data to unrelated third parties. Information is shared only with the recipients listed above, or where required by law, court order, or a competent government authority.

5. Cookies & tracking

This website uses cookies and similar technologies to remember your preferences, understand site usage, and deliver relevant advertising, including essential, analytics, and remarketing cookies from providers such as Google and Meta. You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings; doing so may limit some features of this website.

6. WhatsApp & call communication

If you contact us via WhatsApp, click-to-call, or a form on this site, you consent to being contacted back on the same number by our team or the developer's sales representatives regarding your enquiry, even if your number is registered under NDNC/DND, as this is a transactional response to a request you initiated.

7. Data retention

We retain enquiry data only as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes above and meet legal or reporting obligations. You may request earlier deletion at any time.

8. Security

We use reasonable safeguards, including SSL/TLS encryption on our forms, to protect your data. However, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request a copy of the data we hold, ask us to correct it, withdraw consent to marketing, request deletion, or opt out of remarketing ads via your browser or ad-platform settings. Contact us using the details below to exercise these rights.

10. Children's privacy

This website is intended for individuals aged 18 and above capable of entering into a property enquiry or transaction. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy periodically. The effective date above reflects the latest revision. Continued use of this website after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
