Hero Homes Greater Noida – Comprehensive Project Overview & Key Stats

What is Hero Homes Greater Noida?

Hero Homes Greater Noida is a 17.3-acre landmark residential development by Hero Realty inside the DMIC Integrated Industrial Township in Greater Noida. The project features luxury 2, 3, and 4 BHK apartments with 30+ resort amenities.

What is the starting price for apartments?

Prices start at ₹2.22 Cr* for 3 BHK luxury residences. Special pre-launch rates and early-bird benefits are currently available for initial registrations.

What unit configurations are available?

The development offers 2 BHK, 3 BHK, 3 BHK + Servant, and 4 BHK luxury pent-residences ranging from ~1250 sq. ft. to 2600+ sq. ft.

How far is Jewar Airport (Noida International Airport)?

The Noida International Airport at Jewar is approximately 25 minutes away via the signal-free Yamuna Expressway corridor.

What is the RERA status of the project?

RERA registration for Hero Homes Greater Noida is currently under process. All promotional updates will be synchronized as soon as the RERA number is issued.

Which banks offer home loan pre-approvals?

Home loans are supported by major financial institutions including State Bank of India (SBI), HDFC Bank, ICICI Bank, Axis Bank, and PNB Housing.

Who is developing the project?

The project is developed by Hero Realty Private Limited, the real estate arm of the $50 Billion+ Hero Group conglomerate.
