What is Hero Homes Greater Noida?

Who is the developer of Hero Homes Greater Noida?

Hero Homes Greater Noida is developed by Hero Realty, the real estate arm of the Hero Group, one of India's established business conglomerates known for its manufacturing and engineering heritage.

Where exactly is Hero Homes Sector MU located?

How big is the Hero Homes Greater Noida project?

Hero Homes Sector MU is built on a 4.5-acre site near the larger 17.3-acre DMIC township, comprising three residential towers built to a G+31 storey format with only six apartments per floor.

What configurations are available at Hero Homes Greater Noida?

Hero Homes offers three 3 BHK configurations: 1,650 sq. ft. (3 BHK + 2 Toilets), 1,900 sq. ft. (3 BHK + 3 Toilets), and 2,200 sq. ft. (3 BHK + Servant Room).

Hero Homes Greater Noida is currently in the pre-launch stage. RERA registration is listed as 'Coming Soon' and possession is indicated as 2031 onwards in phased tower handovers.

Hero Homes Greater Noida — Pricing & Cost FAQs

What is the starting price of Hero Homes Greater Noida?

The starting price is ₹2.22 Cr* for the 3 BHK + 2 Toilets configuration (1,650 sq. ft. super built-up area), based on current pre-launch indicative rates.

What is the price of the largest 3 BHK configuration?

The largest configuration — 3 BHK + Servant Room at 2,200 sq. ft. — is priced starting from ₹2.97 Cr*, while the mid-size 1,900 sq. ft. 3 BHK + 3 Toilets unit starts at ₹2.56 Cr*.

What is the price per square foot at Hero Homes Sector MU?

The indicative pre-launch base rate works out to approximately ₹13,500 per sq. ft.* on super built-up area, uniform across all three configurations.

Is GST included in the quoted price?

No. Goods and Services Tax (GST) is applicable separately at 5% without Input Tax Credit (ITC) for under-construction residential units in this segment, and is added on top of the base price.

Are stamp duty and registration charges included in the price?

No. Stamp duty (typically around 5–7% of property value) and government registration charges are extra, payable at the time of final registry execution upon possession.

What are PLC (Preferential Location Charges) at Hero Homes?

Units facing central green parks, the clubhouse, or higher floors attract Preferential Location Charges of roughly ₹150 to ₹400 per sq. ft., depending on tower position and orientation.

What is the token/booking amount to reserve a unit?

The expression of interest (EOI) or token booking amount typically starts from ₹5 Lakhs to ₹10 Lakhs, payable via bank cheque or digital transfer to secure unit allotment.

What is the estimated monthly maintenance cost?

Monthly maintenance is projected at approximately ₹3.00 to ₹4.50 per sq. ft., covering 24/7 security, clubhouse upkeep, common-area power backup, and landscaping.

Hero Homes Greater Noida — RERA, Legal & Possession FAQs

Is Hero Homes Greater Noida RERA registered?

RERA registration for Hero Homes Greater Noida is currently under process and listed as 'Coming Soon.' Buyers should verify the current registration status on the official UP RERA portal before booking.

What is the expected possession date?

Possession is currently indicated as 2031 onwards, to be executed through phased tower handovers, subject to construction progress and final RERA-approved timelines.

What happens if the project gets delayed?

Under RERA regulations, developers are required to compensate buyers for possession delays beyond the RERA-committed date, typically through interest payments as specified in the builder-buyer agreement.

Is it safe to book a unit before RERA registration?

Booking before RERA registration carries additional risk since the project's registration number, approved plans, and legally binding possession date aren't yet finalized — buyers should treat pre-launch bookings as provisional and verify all documentation carefully.

What legal documents should I verify before booking?

Key documents to verify include the RERA registration certificate, approved building plans, title deed/land ownership records, and the builder-buyer agreement, ideally reviewed with legal assistance before making any payment.

Can I get a refund if I cancel my booking?

Refund terms depend on the specific booking agreement and the stage of cancellation; RERA rules generally require developers to refund amounts with interest for developer-side defaults, but buyer-initiated cancellations may attract deductions — confirm exact terms in your allotment letter.
