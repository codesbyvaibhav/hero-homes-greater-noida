Hero Homes Greater Noida vs Prestige Sec 150 : Comparison

Anyone scanning the Greater Noida and Noida property market this year has probably come across the same two names again and again: Hero Homes and Prestige Sector 150. Both are premium, pre-launch residential developments backed by well-known groups, both sit close to the Noida-Greater Noida Expressway, and both are being pitched as long-term bets for families and investors alike. Naturally, the question that follows is Hero Homes vs Prestige 150 — which one actually deserves a spot on your shortlist?

This guide walks through Hero Homes Greater Noida vs Prestige Sector 150 Noida across location, pricing, floor plans, amenities, and investment potential, so you can judge which project lines up better with your budget, family needs, and goals. A quick note before diving in: because both projects are still in early launch stages, publicly available figures on pricing, unit sizes, and tower counts vary from source to source. Treat every number here as indicative, and confirm final specifications through the official brochure and RERA filing before booking.

Quick answer: Choose Hero Homes Greater Noida if you want a low-density, 3 BHK-only project with a larger share of green space and a longer-horizon bet on the upcoming airport corridor. Choose Prestige Sector 150 if you want a bigger, more established township with 2/3/4 BHK flexibility, a lower entry price, and stronger connectivity to inner Noida today.
  • Tighter budget / smaller unit: Prestige — lower entry price through its 2 BHK option
  • Spacious, low-density 3 BHK home: Hero Homes — 3 BHK-only, ~80% open green space
  • Near-term, lower-risk investment: Prestige — established micro-market, faster resale/rental demand
  • Long-horizon, growth-driven investment: Hero Homes — tied to airport-led and DMIC-led appreciation
  • Developer track record: Hero Realty (Hero Group) vs Prestige Group (300+ delivered projects)
  • Location: Sector MU, Greater Noida vs Sector 150, Noida

Both projects are still in pre-launch stages, so treat the figures above as indicative until confirmed via the official brochure and RERA filing.

Hero Homes vs Prestige Sector 150 at a Glance

At a high level, Hero Homes Greater Noida is a low-density, 3 BHK-only development from Hero Realty in Sector MU, while Prestige Sector 150 Noida — marketed as Prestige Bougainvillea Gardens — is a larger, multi-configuration township from Prestige Group spread across roughly 15 acres in Sector 150. If you're choosing purely by scale, Prestige is the bigger of the two, with more towers and a wider mix of 2, 3, and 4 BHK homes. If you're choosing by exclusivity and low-density planning, Hero Homes tends to edge ahead, with only a handful of towers and far fewer apartments per floor.

Hero Homes Greater Noida Overview

Hero Homes Greater Noida at Sector MU is developed by Hero Realty, the real estate arm of the Hero Group, and is positioned as a resort-style, wellness-focused community. The project is spread across roughly 4.5 to 4.7 acres within a larger planned pocket of Sector MU, with three residential towers and a reported density of around six units per floor — a figure the developer highlights as a point of differentiation from denser projects nearby.

Hero Homes Greater Noida keeps its focus narrow: every home on offer is a 3 BHK, available in a few different carpet-area tiers rather than a spread of unit types. The Hero Homes 3 BHK price reportedly starts in the ₹1.8 Cr to ₹2.2 Cr range for the entry configuration, moving upward for larger layouts. Because the project is still pre-launch, treat these as indicative figures pending RERA registration.

Prestige Sector 150 Noida Overview

Prestige Sector 150 Noida, known formally as Prestige Bougainvillea Gardens, is one of Prestige Group's early residential launches in North India, and it comes with the scale the brand is known for elsewhere in the country. The project sits on approximately 14.83 acres, with reports describing 12 towers rising to around 22 floors and roughly 1,000-plus apartments in total.

Unlike Hero Homes, Prestige offers a broader configuration mix — 2 BHK, 3 BHK, and in some listings 4 BHK homes — with unit sizes reported anywhere from around 1,300 sq ft up to over 3,000 sq ft for the largest layouts. That range gives Prestige more flexibility for buyers who want a smaller entry-level home as well as those chasing a larger family residence.

Hero Homes vs Prestige 150: Location Comparison

Hero Homes location sits in Sector MU, a planned residential and employment pocket of Greater Noida that's part of the larger expressway belt and is close to the DMIC industrial corridor. Hero Homes Sector MU location benefits from wide, government-planned roads and proximity to Pari Chowk and the broader Knowledge Park cluster.

Prestige Sector 150 location, by contrast, is one of Noida's more established, low-congestion residential sectors, positioned directly along the Noida-Greater Noida Expressway and closer to inner Noida's schools, hospitals, and retail hubs. Buyers who prioritise an established neighbourhood with existing social infrastructure may lean toward Prestige, while those comfortable with a newer, still-developing micro-market may find Hero Homes more appealing for its lower current entry point relative to future growth.

Price Comparison

Price is often where the Hero Homes vs Prestige comparison gets decided. Hero Homes Greater Noida price currently starts in the ₹1.8 Cr–₹2.2 Cr bracket for the smallest 3 BHK configuration, working out to a per-square-foot rate reported around ₹13,500 in some listings. Because every unit here is a 3 BHK, there's no lower-ticket entry option — you're buying into the premium bracket from the start.

Prestige Sector 150 price spans a wider band, with some listings quoting figures below ₹1 Cr for compact 1 BHK or 2 BHK units and going up to roughly ₹2.7 Cr–₹2.8 Cr for larger 3 BHK homes. Prestige Sector 150 Noida price data is genuinely inconsistent across portals right now — some quote an average rate near ₹10,000–₹12,000 per sq ft, others quote a straight per-unit figure — so this is one area where a direct cost-sheet request from the sales team is worth doing before you compare on paper.

Taken together, Prestige Sector 150 vs Hero Homes Greater Noida on price comes down to ticket size: Prestige offers a lower entry point through smaller configurations, while Hero Homes' 3 BHK-only lineup keeps its starting price higher but arguably more consistent in positioning.

Floor Plans and Apartment Configurations

Hero Homes floor plan options are deliberately narrow. Reported configurations include a 3 BHK + 2T layout around 1,650 sq ft, a 3 BHK + 3T layout around 1,850–1,900 sq ft (some sources list this closer to 1,900 sq ft), and a larger 3 BHK + Servant Room layout around 2,200 sq ft. The Hero Homes servant room configuration is aimed at buyers who want a dedicated staff or utility space, a detail that tends to matter more to larger families than to first-time buyers.

Prestige Sector 150 floor plan choices are broader by design. The Prestige Sector 150 2 BHK option suits smaller households or first-time buyers, the Prestige Sector 150 3 BHK layout covers the mid-market family segment, and select listings mention a Prestige Sector 150 4 BHK option for buyers wanting a larger residence or a sky-villa style layout. Reported sizes span from roughly 1,300 sq ft at the smaller end to well over 3,000 sq ft for the top-tier homes.

Amenities and Lifestyle

Hero Homes amenities are built around the "resort-urban" positioning the developer uses in its marketing — think a large central garden occupying a significant share of the land parcel, a sizeable clubhouse, swimming pool, gymnasium, yoga areas, jogging tracks, and multi-tier security. Several listings put open green space at around 80% of the total land, a figure the developer ties directly to the low tower count and reduced units-per-floor design.

Prestige Sector 150 amenities lean toward scale rather than exclusivity: a multi-level clubhouse, one of the larger banquet halls reported in the Delhi NCR region, swimming pools on multiple towers, sports courts, a kids' play zone, yoga and wellness decks, and landscaped gardens across the township. Both projects lead with wellness and family-oriented lifestyle features, but Hero Homes markets itself on lower density and more breathing room per resident, while Prestige leans on the sheer variety and volume of its shared facilities.

Connectivity and Surrounding Infrastructure

On paper, both addresses benefit from the same broad expressway network, but the emphasis differs. Hero Homes Sector MU connectivity is anchored by direct access to the expressway and reasonable proximity to the Yamuna Expressway, positioning it as a reasonably short drive from the upcoming Jewar Airport — also referred to as Noida International Airport in official communication. Sector MU Greater Noida also sits within reach of the Aqua Line Metro corridor that links Noida and Greater Noida.

Sector 150 connectivity is arguably the stronger of the two for daily Noida commuters, sitting directly along the same expressway with closer access to the Sector 148 Metro Station and established parts of the city. For buyers weighing Delhi connectivity specifically, Prestige's inner-Noida location currently offers a shorter, more predictable commute, while Hero Homes' Sector MU address is better positioned for the long-term airport corridor and Knowledge Park 2 Metro Station catchment as that infrastructure matures. Overall Greater Noida connectivity continues to improve on both fronts as expressway widening and metro extension projects progress.

Which Project Is Better for Families?

Families evaluating Noida vs Greater Noida as a broader question will find both projects make a reasonable case. Prestige suits families who want an established neighbourhood today — existing schools, hospitals, and retail within easier reach, plus the flexibility of 2 BHK and 3 BHK options depending on family size. Hero Homes suits families who value low-density living, larger green cover, and a project designed from the ground up around wellness, and who are comfortable with a slightly longer runway as the surrounding Sector MU infrastructure develops.

Which Project Is Better for Investors?

Greater Noida vs Noida is also a live debate among investors, and each project draws on a different growth story. Hero Homes investment potential rests heavily on its proximity to the Jewar Airport corridor and the broader DMIC development plan — areas that are expected to see meaningful infrastructure-led appreciation over the next several years, though that upside is inherently tied to how quickly those projects actually get delivered.

Prestige Sector 150 investment potential leans on a more immediate growth story: an established micro-market, a nationally recognised developer with a long delivery track record, and steadier near-term rental and resale demand given Sector 150's existing residential base. Investors who prefer a longer-horizon, higher-risk-higher-reward bet may lean toward Hero Homes, while those who prefer a more predictable, near-term investment case may find Prestige the safer allocation.

Hero Homes vs Prestige Sector 150: Key Differences

A few differences stand out when you line the two up side by side. Hero Homes Greater Noida Sector MU is a smaller, 3 BHK-focused project built around low density and a large share of open space, backed by the Hero Group's brand entering real estate. Prestige Sector 150 Noida is a larger, multi-configuration township from an established national developer, offering more choice in unit size and a more mature surrounding neighbourhood.

Price-wise, Hero Homes starts higher because it skips smaller configurations entirely, while Prestige offers a lower entry ticket through its 2 BHK options. On location, Hero Homes bets on the future of Sector MU and the airport catchment, while Prestige already sits inside a more developed part of Noida with shorter commute times today.

Which Project Fits Which Type of Buyer?

  • First-time buyers on a tighter budget may find Prestige Sector 150's smaller configurations more accessible than Hero Homes' 3 BHK-only lineup.
  • Buyers who specifically want a spacious, low-density 3 BHK with a strong emphasis on green space will likely prefer Hero Homes.
  • Growing families wanting flexibility between 2, 3, and 4 BHK homes within the same address may lean toward Prestige.
  • Long-horizon investors betting on airport-led growth may find Hero Homes' Sector MU address more compelling.
  • Investors wanting steadier, near-term demand in an established sector may prefer Prestige's established base here.

Final Verdict: Hero Homes or Prestige Sector 150?

There isn't a single right answer to Hero Homes vs Prestige 150 which is better — it depends on what you're optimising for. If low density, larger green cover, and a focused 3 BHK product matter most, Hero Homes Sector MU Greater Noida is the stronger fit. If you want configuration flexibility, a more established neighbourhood, and a developer with a long national delivery record, Prestige Sector 150 Noida is likely the better choice.

Whichever way you're leaning, don't finalise a booking on marketing figures alone. Request the latest cost sheet, confirm RERA registration status for both projects, and, where possible, do a site visit before signing anything — pre-launch pricing and specifications on projects like these tend to shift as construction progresses.
