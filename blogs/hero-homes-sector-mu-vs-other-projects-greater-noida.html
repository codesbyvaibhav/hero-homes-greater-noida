Hero Homes Sector MU vs Other Projects in Greater Noida

Greater Noida's premium housing market has never had this much choice. Alongside the Yamuna Expressway corridor and the established Noida–Greater Noida Expressway belt, buyers today are comparing Hero Homes Greater Noida against projects from Gaursons, ACE Group, ATS, Eldeco, Supertech, and Migsun — often within a 20–30 minute drive of each other. If you're shortlisting a 3 BHK home in this price bracket, the decision usually comes down to five things: location, price per sq ft, amenities and floor plans, connectivity, and possession timeline. This guide compares Hero Homes Sector MU Greater Noida against the other prominent projects currently active in the region, so you can see exactly where it fits before you book a site visit.

Quick answer: Hero Homes Sector MU Greater Noida is priced at approximately ₹13,500 per sq ft for spacious 3 BHK configurations (1,650–2,200 sq ft) starting from ₹2.22 Cr*. This is above the Greater Noida city average of roughly ₹8,100 per sq ft, but it competes directly with Yamuna Expressway launches like ATS Allure, Eldeco Echoes of Eden, and ACE Terra, priced ₹8,500–₹13,000 per sq ft. Hero Homes Greater Noida differentiates through Hero Realty's backing, low floor density (6 units/floor), 80% open green space, and a 50+ amenity clubhouse, while Yamuna Expressway rivals offer closer Jewar Airport proximity at a lower entry price.

Why Location Matters More Than the Sector Number

Greater Noida isn't one homogenous market — it's several distinct corridors, and pricing reflects that:

  • Sector MU, Greater Noida: A large, government-planned residential and employment zone with industrial plots already allotted to companies like Haier, active road-widening projects, and improving connectivity to Jewar Airport. Hero Homes Sector MU sits within this planned zone.
  • Yamuna Expressway corridor (Sectors 17A, 19, 22A, 22D): The dominant zone for new premium launches — ACE, ATS, Eldeco, Gaursons, Supertech, and Migsun all have active projects here, generally 7–15 km from Jewar Airport.
  • Noida–Greater Noida Expressway / Greater Noida West: More mature, higher base prices in central Noida, lower entry points in Greater Noida West sectors.

Because Hero Homes Greater Noida sits within a planned employment-linked zone rather than a purely residential sector, it offers a different value proposition — job proximity and planned infrastructure — compared to the largely residential Yamuna Expressway belt.

Hero Homes Greater Noida vs Eldeco Echoes of Eden

Hero Homes Sector MU Greater Noida offers exclusively 3 BHK configurations across 1,650–2,200 sq ft, priced at roughly ₹13,500 per sq ft with a starting price of ₹2.22 Cr*. The development spans 4.5 acres with 3 towers, a G+31 storey structure, and 6 units per floor, part of Hero Realty's larger 17.3-acre presence in Sector MU. Possession is slated for 2031 onwards.

Eldeco Echoes of Eden, developed by the NSE/BSE-listed Eldeco Group in Sector 22D on the Yamuna Expressway, offers 2, 3, and 4 BHK configurations at a launch price of around ₹8,999 per sq ft — notably lower than Hero Homes Sector MU. It sits roughly 7 km from Jewar Airport and is UP RERA registered. Buyers who want configuration flexibility and closer airport access may lean toward Eldeco; buyers who prioritize low floor density and a wellness-focused master plan will find Hero Homes Greater Noida the stronger fit.

Hero Homes Greater Noida vs ATS Allure

ATS Allure is ATS Group's premium pre-launch offering in Sector 22D on the Yamuna Expressway, with 3 and 4 BHK configurations. ATS carries a strong finishing and construction-quality reputation in NCR, making it a direct alternative for buyers who weight build quality over a listed-developer balance sheet. As a pre-launch project, firm pricing and possession dates are still emerging.

Against this, Hero Homes Sector MU Greater Noida offers a more defined pricing structure at ₹13,500 per sq ft and a clearer possession timeline of 2031 onwards, along with Hero Realty's parent-group backing. Buyers who prefer more certainty on price and timeline today may find Hero Homes Greater Noida the more predictable choice while ATS Allure's final positioning is still taking shape.

Hero Homes Greater Noida vs Gaur Yamuna City

Gaur Yamuna City, developed by Gaursons India in Sector 19 on the Yamuna Expressway, is backed by the developer with the highest premium-segment transaction volume in Greater Noida. It offers 3 and 4 BHK units, with some phases already ready-to-move — a meaningful advantage for buyers who don't want to wait years for possession.

Hero Homes Sector MU Greater Noida, by comparison, is a newer, larger-scale launch with a longer possession horizon but a lower floor density (6 units per floor versus higher-density towers common in established townships) and a larger 80% open-greens allocation. Buyers wanting immediate or near-term possession and an established resale market will lean toward Gaur Yamuna City; buyers prioritizing low-density living and long-term appreciation will lean toward Hero Homes Greater Noida.

Hero Homes Greater Noida vs ACE Terra / ACE Verde

ACE Group's Terra and Verde projects in Sectors 22A/22D on the Yamuna Expressway offer 3 BHK and 3 BHK + Servant configurations (roughly 1,800–2,400 sq ft), with amenities comparable to Hero Homes' clubhouse-and-wellness positioning. These projects are under construction, generally priced below Hero Homes Sector MU's per-sq-ft rate, with closer proximity to Jewar Airport.

Hero Homes Greater Noida counters with a stronger green-space ratio, lower floor density, and a more expansive 50+ amenity clubhouse. For buyers weighing airport proximity and a lower entry price against more open space and a larger master-planned community, this is one of the closer contests in the comparison.

Hero Homes Greater Noida vs Supertech Up Country

Supertech Up Country, in Sector 17A on the Yamuna Expressway, is an established, larger township offering 2, 3, and 4 BHK apartments with phased delivery — some inventory already delivered. It suits buyers prioritizing a longer developer history and near-term possession over new-launch specifications.

Hero Homes Sector MU Greater Noida, as a newer entrant, cannot yet match that delivery track record, but it offers a differentiated positioning — exclusively spacious 3 BHK layouts, lower floor density, and Hero Realty's broader industrial-group backing. Buyers who value a longer operating history in the same micro-market may prefer Supertech; buyers who value newer specifications and lower-density living may prefer Hero Homes Greater Noida.

Amenities and Floor Plan Comparison

Amenities across this price segment look broadly similar on paper — clubhouse, pool, gym, landscaped gardens, kids' play areas, and multi-tier security appear in nearly every brochure in this bracket. The differences that matter show up in the details.

Hero Homes Greater Noida lists 50+ facilities, including a 25,000+ sq ft clubhouse, an infinity-edge pool, a coworking lounge, aromatherapy gardens, EV charging bays, and 5-tier smart security with biometric access. Its floor plans emphasize wide balconies (three to four per unit), cross-ventilation, and Vastu-compliant layouts, with carpet-to-super-area efficiency around 71–72% across its three configurations.

Yamuna Expressway projects generally match on clubhouse and security features but vary more on unit efficiency and balcony count depending on tower design. Higher floor-density towers (8–10+ units per floor) common in some Yamuna Expressway launches can mean narrower common areas and longer lift waits compared to Hero Homes Sector MU's 6-units-per-floor layout. Buyers should always check the carpet-to-super-area ratio on the RERA filing rather than the brochure, since this varies meaningfully even within the same price band.

Connectivity Comparison

Connectivity is where the two corridors diverge most clearly. Hero Homes Sector MU Greater Noida benefits from proximity to the Noida-Greater Noida Expressway (around 5 minutes' drive), Pari Chowk and the Depot Metro Station (around 8 minutes), and reputed schools and hospitals within a 10–12 minute zone, alongside a nearby industrial employment base.

Yamuna Expressway projects are built around the expressway itself — offering faster, more direct access to Jewar Airport (commercial operations began mid-2026) and Agra, but generally sitting farther from established metro stations and mature social infrastructure, much of which is still being built out around newer sectors. Buyers who commute daily to Noida or Delhi may find Hero Homes Greater Noida's expressway-and-metro-adjacent location shorter and more predictable; buyers prioritizing air travel access or appreciation tied to the airport's economic zone will find the Yamuna Expressway corridor holds an edge.

Hero Homes Sector MU: Where It Wins

1. Planned township backing. Sector MU is a government-planned residential and employment zone with active infrastructure investment — road widening and industrial plot allotments are already underway, not merely proposed.

2. Low floor density. At 6 units per floor across a G+31 structure on 4.5 acres, Hero Homes Greater Noida offers more privacy per floor than many high-density Yamuna Expressway towers, some of which run 8–10+ units per floor.

3. Open green space ratio. An 80% open-greens plan is on the higher end for the region; several competing high-rise projects allocate less open land relative to tower footprint.

4. Employment-hub proximity by design. With industrial plots and operational manufacturing units nearby, residents of Hero Homes Sector MU may see shorter commutes to jobs generated locally — a different thesis than pure residential corridors offer.

5. Developer diversification. Hero Realty operates under Hero Enterprise, an industrial conglomerate with 15+ years in real estate and 3.5M+ sq ft delivered — relevant for buyers who weight parent-company strength alongside real estate track record.

Where Other Greater Noida Projects Win

1. Airport proximity. Several Yamuna Expressway projects (Eldeco Echoes of Eden, ATS Allure, ACE Verde) sit roughly 7–15 km from Jewar Airport, closer than Hero Homes Sector MU.

2. Lower entry price. Yamuna Expressway launches starting around ₹8,500–₹9,000 per sq ft undercut Hero Homes Greater Noida's ~₹13,500 per sq ft by a meaningful margin.

3. More configuration choice. Many competing projects offer 2, 3, and 4 BHK options, versus Hero Homes Sector MU's exclusively 3 BHK lineup — useful for buyers wanting a smaller entry unit or a larger family home.

4. Established developer footprint. Gaursons leads Greater Noida's premium transaction volume by both value and count, giving buyers more resale comparables to evaluate.

5. Ready or near-ready inventory. Certain Gaur Yamuna City and Supertech Up Country phases are already delivered or nearing completion, compared to Hero Homes Sector MU's 2031-onwards timeline — significant for buyers who can't wait 5+ years.

Investment Potential: A Longer View

Property values across both corridors are driven by the same macro triggers — Jewar Airport's commercial launch, continued industrial investment in the region, and expanding expressway and metro connectivity. Where they differ is the shape of the investment case. Yamuna Expressway pricing has already moved up sharply as airport operations began, meaning some appreciation is already priced in for new buyers entering today. Hero Homes Sector MU Greater Noida, as part of a still-maturing planned zone, represents an earlier-stage bet: pricing reflects proximity to a nearby employment base and infrastructure spend still being built out — the profile long-term investors typically look for before a location matures. Buyers weighing rental yield sooner may lean toward corridors with faster possession and established tenant demand; buyers weighing capital appreciation over 5–10 years may find Hero Homes Greater Noida's positioning more compelling, provided they're comfortable with the longer timeline.

How to Decide Between Them

  • Choose Hero Homes Sector MU Greater Noida if: you want a planned residential and employment zone, prefer lower floor density and higher green cover, are comfortable with a longer possession horizon (2031+), and are buying for long-term capital appreciation rather than immediate move-in.
  • Choose a Yamuna Expressway project if: airport proximity is your top priority, you want a lower entry price, you need 2 BHK or 4 BHK options, or you want ready/near-ready inventory.
  • Choose an established Noida–Greater Noida Expressway address if: you want mature social infrastructure (schools, hospitals, malls already operational) over new-launch upside, and don't mind central-Noida pricing.

The Bottom Line

Hero Homes Greater Noida isn't competing on price — it's competing on planned-township scale, developer diversification, and long-term infrastructure conviction in Sector MU. Buyers wanting the lowest entry cost or fastest possession will likely find better-fitting options on the Yamuna Expressway corridor. Buyers prioritizing low-density living, larger green cover, richer amenities, and a differentiated investment thesis will find Hero Homes Sector MU Greater Noida the stronger long-term fit.

As with any pre-launch purchase, confirm current RERA status, verified pricing, floor plans, and payment plans directly with each project's sales team before deciding — asking prices in this market shift with every construction milestone.
