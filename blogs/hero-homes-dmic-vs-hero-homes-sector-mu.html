Hero Homes DMIC vs Hero Homes Sector MU: What's the Difference?

If you've searched "Hero Homes Greater Noida" and landed on results mentioning both Hero Homes DMIC and Hero Homes Sector MU, you're not alone — and you're not looking at a mistake. These are two separate residential developments by Hero Realty, sharing a brand name and a city, but differing in location, scale, configuration, and launch stage.

This guide breaks down exactly how the two projects differ, so you can quickly identify which one matches what you're actually looking for.

Quick answer: Hero Homes DMIC and Hero Homes Sector MU are two separate residential projects by Hero Realty in Greater Noida — not the same development. Hero Homes Sector MU is a 4.5-acre, 3-tower project offering premium 3 BHK apartments starting at ₹2.22 Cr* in Sector MU. Hero Homes DMIC is a larger 17.3-acre township-style development located inside Greater Noida's Delhi-Mumbai Industrial Corridor (DMIC) Integrated Industrial Township. They share a developer and a city, but differ in location, scale, configuration, and launch stage.

Are Hero Homes DMIC and Hero Homes Sector MU the Same Project?

No. They are two distinct projects developed under the same Hero Realty brand, in the same broader Greater Noida region, which is why the names get confused in search results. Think of them as sibling projects, not the same address:

  • Hero Homes Sector MU sits within Sector MU, Greater Noida — a defined residential sector.
  • Hero Homes DMIC is located inside the DMIC Integrated Industrial Township, a larger planned corridor zone that includes residential, commercial, and industrial land use.

Both are marketed by Hero Realty, both target the growing Greater Noida corridor, and both benefit from proximity to the upcoming Noida International Airport (Jewar) — but they are independent developments with separate land parcels, separate specifications, and separate pricing.

Location: How Far Apart Are They?

Both projects fall within Greater Noida's rapidly developing western corridor, but they sit in different planning zones. Hero Homes Sector MU is positioned within the established Sector MU residential belt, close to the Noida-Greater Noida Expressway and Pari Chowk. Hero Homes DMIC sits inside the dedicated DMIC Integrated Industrial Township — a larger, purpose-planned zone built around India's Delhi-Mumbai Industrial Corridor initiative, which combines residential pockets with commercial and industrial infrastructure.

In practical terms: Sector MU suits buyers who want a conventional, close-knit residential tower community. DMIC suits buyers or investors thinking about a larger, employment-hub-adjacent township with longer-term infrastructure upside tied to industrial corridor growth.

Scale and Development Type

This is the clearest structural difference between the two.

Hero Homes Sector MU is a focused, low-density residential project — 3 towers on 4.5 acres, capped at 6 units per floor. It's designed around exclusivity and open green space (80% open greens), not scale.

Hero Homes DMIC is a township-scale development spanning 17.3 acres — nearly four times the land parcel — positioned as a larger, multi-phase community within an industrial corridor zone rather than a single-tower residential pocket.

If you're comparing the two on "which is bigger," DMIC is the larger land parcel by a wide margin. If you're comparing "which is more exclusive/low-density," Sector MU is the tighter, boutique offering.

Pricing and Configuration

Hero Homes Sector MU currently offers three confirmed 3 BHK configurations:

  • 3 BHK + 2 Toilets (1,650 sq ft) — from ₹2.22 Cr*
  • 3 BHK + 3 Toilets (1,900 sq ft) — from ₹2.56 Cr*
  • 3 BHK + Servant Room (2,200 sq ft) — from ₹2.97 Cr*

Hero Homes DMIC's configurations and pricing are yet to be officially released. Because DMIC is a larger township development, expect a broader mix of unit types once details are announced, rather than a single exclusive configuration band like Sector MU.

Which One Should You Consider?

  • Choose Hero Homes Sector MU if: you want a smaller, low-density gated community, defined 3 BHK options, and a project already at an advanced pre-launch stage with published pricing.
  • Choose Hero Homes DMIC if: you're taking a longer-term investment view tied to industrial corridor growth, are open to a township-scale development, and are comfortable waiting for configuration and pricing details to be finalized.
