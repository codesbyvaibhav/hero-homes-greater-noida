Step-by-Step: How to Book a Flat at Hero Homes Sector Mu

The booking process at Hero Homes Sector Mu follows a fairly standard structure used across most RERA-governed residential projects in Greater Noida. Here's what it typically involves:

1. Shortlist your configuration — Decide between the 1,650 sq ft, 1,900 sq ft, or 2,200 sq ft layout based on your family size and budget.

2. Request the latest price list and cost sheet — Contact the authorized sales team to get updated pricing, floor availability, and any active pre-launch offers.

3. Schedule a site visit — A guided visit lets you inspect sample layouts, understand tower orientation, and evaluate the neighborhood before committing.

4. Complete KYC documentation — This includes a PAN card, identity proof (Aadhaar/Passport/Voter ID), address proof, passport-size photographs, and payment for the booking amount.

5. Pay the booking amount — This secures your preferred unit and locks in the current price before any escalation.

6. Review the payment plan — Confirm whether a construction-linked or milestone-based schedule applies to your unit before signing anything.

7. Sign the allotment letter and builder-buyer agreement — Once documentation and initial payment are complete, the developer issues an allotment letter, followed by the formal agreement.

8. Track construction and possession updates — Possession is currently indicated for 2031 onwards*, so staying in touch with the sales/CRM team for construction updates is recommended.
